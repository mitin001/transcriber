# [When the markets need a designer](https://www.npr.org/transcripts/1257189367)

[Regulations] are supposed to protect society from a business that gets so powerful that it begins to take unfair advantage its customers. However, even in a regulated market not every business has the same advantages. To compare businesses to each other in terms of the degree of their advantages, we look at their market power.

> Market power is when a single market participant can influence the outcomes of the market in their favor at the expense of everyone else.

One business may have such high market power that it causes the tragedy of the commons.

> The tragedy of the commons happens when market participants who are acting in their self-interest deplete a shared resource in a way that means that's against their own community outcomes.

When a fisherman catches all the fish in a lake, he causes the tragedy of the commons. There will not be fish in this lake for anyone else to catch. Not only will other fishermen suffer but also the whole ecosystem of the lake will be disrupted by a sudden disappearance of one of its most important species.
