# [Why I joined DOGE](https://www.npr.org/transcripts/1254121714)

How could you watch Elon Musk's pathetic antics and pseudointellectual musings all these years and still not be convinced he's nothing more than an attention-seeking bullshitter? It took one DOGE staffer asking him a direct question to get on board with the rest of us. Sahil Lavingia asked Musk about his outsider's perspective on the inner workings of government.

> What are you learning about how the federal government works, basically?

Elon Musk's answer was exactly the kind of nonsense you would expect.

> It's just like a fractal of terribleness. And you just can't believe how terrible it is until you peel back the next layer, and it's even more terrible.

Sahil didn't expect that, though. Musk's bullshit left him deeply disappointed.

> That doesn't help me do my job. I was expecting a little bit more of a concrete answer.

Don't expect this genius to save humanity. He won't bring the end of gasoline-powered cars. He won't land a rocket on Mars. He won't put a chip into your brain. His companies are done. Without government subsidies and public support, their competition will easily drive them off the market. Neither this President nor the next one will have a relationship with Musk. This genius burns bridges wherever he goes.
