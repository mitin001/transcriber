# [Trade deficits](https://www.npr.org/transcripts/1243652738)

If the US has a trade deficit with a foreign country, it means that that foreign country has bought less in terms of goods and services from the US than the US has bought from them. In other words, it has a surplus of US dollars. Everything balances out in the economy, and this balances out with them investing these dollars back into the US economy by buying stock in American companies. 

> If they're not using those dollars to buy our stuff, then the only thing left to do is invest those dollars in the United States.

Or, they do a currency exchange thereby selling the US dollars to a country that then buys US goods or stocks. Most countries want in on the US economic growth, and so most countries prefer to invest in American companies over buying American goods. Either way, the US comes out the winner as long as nothing stands in the way of foreign trade or foreign investment. This is why imposing tariffs proportional to trade deficits was a stupid idea and resulted in the shrinking of the US economy.
