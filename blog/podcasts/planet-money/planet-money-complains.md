# [Planet Money complains](https://www.npr.org/transcripts/1248091506)

Coffeeshops don't give us free refills because of our hedonic adaptation.

> You don't just go up, up, up in happiness forever. For instance, as you make money and you can buy more and more things, your expectations also rise. This seems like a good reason for a coffee shop to not give free refills because they're like, _oh, whatever, you're just going to get mad at us about something later on anyway_.

Why should they waste money giving us free coffee when this won't make us happy anyway? As soon as a person reaches a certain level in any part of life, staying at this level no longer gives us joy. We're only happy as we move up levels. Change is good. Change is happiness.
