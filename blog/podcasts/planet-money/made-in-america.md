# [Made in America](https://www.npr.org/transcripts/1255526971)

Luxury brands pay its workers the same as discount brands.

> A $20 t-shirt, a $120 t-shirt, the workers likely got $0.20 to work on it either way.

Regardless of how much the shirt cost the consumer, the worker who made it got paid the same: the [minimum] the corporation could pay them.

> You generally cannot buy your way into better wages for workers.

Whereas it's legal for corporations to stiff workers on their wages, they also absolutely do illegal things. Especially because the price of illegality is so low. A simple rebrand.

> If they were caught doing anything and the government came in, they would say, sorry, that company no longer exists. We're this owner. We're the new company now.
