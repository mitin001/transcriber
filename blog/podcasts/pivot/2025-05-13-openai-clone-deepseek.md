# [2025-05-13](https://s3.amazonaws.com/writecomments.com/transcripts/0ccbc3c572466771f27c4721a882a70d.csv): OpenAI has a market differentiation problem

OpenAI set itself up for getting cloned, and now its clone has driven down the price of its shares and the hype of AI along with it.

> It is very difficult for OpenAI—for any AI—to establish technological differentiation because AI just crawls it.

DeepSeek is the Chinese offbrand of ChatGPT. Instead of finding their own training data like OpenAI, they just trained their AI model on the output of ChatGPT.

> When you asked DeepSeek what LLM it was, for the first 10 days it said, "I'm OpenAI."

The lesson is that if you make a product and want market differentiation for it, make it hard to reverse-engineer it.

> These things reverse-engineer each other.

You would think that decades of Chinese IP thievery would teach American companies this lesson.
