# [Something Bizarre Is Happening to People Who Use ChatGPT a Lot](https://futurism.com/the-byte/chatgpt-dependence-addiction)

OpenAI's own study has found a subset of users addicted to their AI bot.

> ChatGPT "power users," or those who use it the most and at the longest durations, are becoming dependent upon — or even addicted to — the chatbot.

The business model of sports betting apps depends on the tiny percentage of their users, the gambling addicts. Now that the hype for ChatGPT is dying down, perhaps OpenAI has recognized such users as _their_ profit drivers as well and so the company publishes a 50-page study burying this finding in it and dismissing it as insignificant before journalists start sounding the alarm.
