# [2025-04-25](https://s3.amazonaws.com/writecomments.com/transcripts/2bbdf45d99f6e56c1e020254987d4cb7.csv): Invest in boring, not sexy. Negotiate unemotionally. Do not fear failure

If you find yourself in a role of a negotiator, Scott Galloway has advice for you. A negotiator must have excellent control of their emotions, and a satisfactory way out in case the negotiation does not work out. You cannot enter a negotiation with the mindset that failure is not an option. Arrange your life in such a way that for every negotiation, there's a satisfactory alternative in case the negotiation doesn't work out.

> The only two things you have to remember in a negotiation are not to make it emotional, not to have it be a win-lose, and to always show a credible willingness to walk away.

Invest in boring things. Only 1% of people make decent returns on things that are sexy. Do not invest in something that's already over-invested. The trick behind economic prosperity is finding something people find necessary but boring.

> The sexier the business, the lower the ROI. If someone wants you to invest in a members club downtown for artists and creatives, join the club, do not invest. If someone wants you to invest in software as a service for scheduling healthcare maintenance workers, if it sounds so fucking awful you wouldn't want to work there, that's where you write a check. Because anything that's sexy is over-invested, which drives down returns. The less sexy, the more return on your invested capital. Also, think about that in terms of your career. If you're going for something sexy, you better get bright green lights that you're in the top 1%, otherwise find something boring. This is what creates passion, mastery, artisanship, and be able to take care of your kids and your parents.

If you've found a flow of capital for which no one is competing, that gives you an edge. Capture this flow. The hunter-gatherer model of human survival still applies today. Hunt for hidden waterfalls of cash and go there every day to refill.
