# [2025-08-16](https://writecomments.com/transcripts/?md5=da0e7098f185a90de9c1d39b20927bb6): Thomas Chatterton Williams on progress in social justice

The fact that social justice warriors get so worked up over microaggressions is a good sign.

> As society becomes more equal, even the smallest inequalities become more and more unbearable.

In other words, we are so close to all being equal in the eyes of our society that even the tiniest hints of discrimination stick out.

> My father grew up in segregated Texas. Microaggressions were not a thing back then. The lynchings were a thing. My father was born in 1937.

Achieving this much progress in under a century is a cause for celebration.
