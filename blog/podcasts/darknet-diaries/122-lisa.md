# [122](https://darknetdiaries.com/transcript/122/). Lisa

The attacker will always have the advantage of not playing by the rules or being constrained by law.

> Attackers don’t stick to what’s legal or play by the rules, yet defenders do have to remain legal on how they defend, which gives these kind of battles a type of asymmetry in how the battle is waged.

The defender must find other advantages, like strength in numbers. As long as the law-abiding community far outweighs the lawless in its size, the will be enough force to counteract the anarchist advantages of the attackers.
