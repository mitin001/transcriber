# [95](https://darknetdiaries.com/transcript/95/). Jon & Brian's Big Adventure

A pentester or threat actor will rub up against you in a coffee shop hoping to clone your badge.

> We followed the employees as they would go around town and then clone their badges as they were waiting in line at a coffee shop or if they were going somewhere for lunch.

The will have a long-range badge NFC reader in their bag, and as soon as it vibrates, the attacker will know to retreat. Mission accomplished.

> If we’re in line in a coffee shop, if I can just step at the right angle to your badge, I can get a read.  So, I’ll just put my phone up; I’ll be on a phone call and kinda pace back and forth as I’m talking on the phone, just naturally as somebody may in a coffee shop, try not to disturb everybody else, and just kinda pace around until I can feel my bag vibrate.

Once they have the readout from the badge, they can create a clone and use it to infiltrate your office.
