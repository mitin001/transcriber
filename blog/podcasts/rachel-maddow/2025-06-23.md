# [2025-06-23](https://s3.amazonaws.com/writecomments.com/transcripts/c4d6f564efefea430a184b8c1ed487c9.csv): Trump shouldn't have the power to declare war

Senator Tim Kaine, Democrat of Virginia, explains why the power to make a decision about declaring war or ceasefire is with Congress, not the President.

> We should not allow war and peace or bombing or ceasefire to be based on the whims of any one person, this president or any one person. And that's why the framers of the Constitution said war should only be declared after a debate and a vote by Congress. It's just too risky to allow one person, Democrat, Republican, Whig or Federalist, to send our sons and daughters to war based upon their whim, vibe or mood of the day.

A declaration of war is a big decision, and, as such, it should be deliberated at length by a group of people.

> The founders made this decision deliberately. They knew it was harder for a group of people to make a decision, a difficult decision about something like war, than it would be for an individual person to make such a decision. And that's explicitly, they explain, that is explicitly why they vested that power in the Congress.

Meanwhile, Trump routinely usurps the authority of Congress. He willy-nilly [shuts down agencies] that were created by Congress. And Congress doesn't bat an eye. This Trump presidency is marked by a [particularly impotent legislative branch]. Trump is about to start declaring wars on random countries at this point.
