# [Rachel Maddow](https://podcastfeeds.nbcnews.com/rachelmaddow)

Rachel is not particularly charitable in her analysis of the Trump administration, and for a good reason. Her extensive academic background in political science allows her to see the second Trump term as a shift in the American form of government from a democratic republic to an oligarchy, a shift destructive to the American ideals that have so far worked well for the benefit of its citizen and the world and still had much unrealized potential. Concluding the American experiment while it's at its peak just because one President was able to exploit the attention economy and was easily bought by foreign entities is a regrettable waste. Rachel is doing her part to try to prevent this: she informs her audience of the implications of the Trump presidency and how to fight his destructive impulses.
