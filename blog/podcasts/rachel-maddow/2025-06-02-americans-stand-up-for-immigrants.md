# [2025-06-02](https://s3.amazonaws.com/writecomments.com/transcripts/3923beb6cf614697c0880675ff9f0526.csv): Americans stand up for immigrants in the face of ICE raids

Trump rally attendees cheer at the idea of being cruel to immigrants, so politicians in the Trump circle thought that if they marketed themselves with cruelty to immigrants, America would just eat that up.

> These guys talked themselves into the idea that the country would be so excited to see them be really performatively cruel to immigrants. They thought this would be a great political advantage for them. We will perform feats of military-like strength, going after immigrants and humiliating them and debasing them and abusing them for public consumption and that will build our political capital. That's what they thought. Turns out everywhere they try it, everyone in America hates it.

Fortunately, America is not a Trump rally. We will not abide such sadistic abuse of the most vulnerable among us. [We will not be fooled]. We understand that if we don't speak up when they come for them, then there won't be anyone left when they [come for us].
