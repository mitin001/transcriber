# [10.05](https://s3.amazonaws.com/writecomments.com/transcripts/89b08cafe39b298909df5d3fd8280c88.csv). Ludacris Has Been Fired

Al Franken sharply criticizes Fox for being an imposter in the business of journalism and news media.

> Fox was this propaganda machine that did not have any integrity, and that's the opposite of what the press has to be.

Fox News would put a self-loathing black man on its programs.

> Jesse Lee Peterson would reliably say what a white racist would say. And he'd say it, you know, full-throated and as a black person.

They put him on to give all the white people on this network permission to spew overtly racist rhetoric at their viewers.

> It serves to inoculate every other white person on Fox News from being called racist because this black guy is saying it.

Fox News was not only racist, but it was also hypocritical. Double standards ran rampant. Its on-air personalities were allowed to misbehave as they pleased while everyone else had to walk a tightrope, never straying from what Fox News wouldn't find objectionable, or else they risked falling victim to its wrath. For example, compare Ludacris to Bill O'Reilly.

> He'd actually written a novel about a newsman violently murdering his colleagues that also included graphic sex scenes. Now he was telling his viewers that Ludacris's rap lyrics encouraged antisocial behavior and degraded women.

Bill O'Reilly went on a crusade against Ludacris and even got Pepsi to drop its endorsement deal with him. All this despite giving the same subjects the same prominence in his work as the rapper he worked so hard to disgrace.
