# [10.02](https://s3.amazonaws.com/writecomments.com/transcripts/c1e14f2afeb330fd6b4d32588b013b4a.csv). A Network for Normal People

Part of the early image of Fox News was that it never labeled itself a right-leaning or conservative news network.

> It couldn't declare what it was. It had to let people decide what it was.

But already people were beginning to notice that the network was cavalier with the truth aspect of the news they were reporting. On January 26, 1997, the Fox network broadcast the Super Bowl for the first time, and during its halftime show, they aired a commercial for Fox News disguised as a breaking news story. 

> We interrupt this program for a Fox News Channel special report.

Fox News employees were beginning to notice it, too.

> That's our bumper music. That's our set.

The news alert was a tired scaremongering trope: a peryson break.

> They cut to Catherine Crier, who was one of the other anchors, and she starts to deliver something about a prison break.

This was presented like a breaking news alert, but in reality it was an advertising stunt, a hoax.

> And what it turns out to be was not a breaking news story.

So, the news channel introduces itself to the public as one that is willing to trade credibility for eyeballs.

> We're a brand-new news operation. We have not even begun to establish our credibility. This is probably the first time that 99% of the viewers even know that Fox News exists.

Establishing legitimacy in the news space was not important to Fox News.

> It was a horrible way to introduce us to America because we were trying to be a legitimate news organization.

The channel has done nothing since then to even try to regain its credibility.

> The concept of credibility continues to befuddle the alleged geniuses who oversee Fox News.

When a journalist confronts his superior about his poor journalistic practices, he gets the boot.

> This is not about journalism. This is about business. We are focused on supplying a part of society that has not gotten what they want.

Fox News exists to validate the lies conservatives tell each other about the world: their conspiracy theories, scapegoating of minority populations, the mean world syndrome, etc.
