# [10.04](https://s3.amazonaws.com/writecomments.com/transcripts/7d13591c1079b68cbdce47d3a85d950d.csv). Beacon of Truth

Charlie Reina posted an [open letter] about the lack of journalistic integrity at Fox News.

> At the Fair and Balanced network, everyone knows management’s point of view, and, in case they’re not sure how to get it on air, The Memo is there to remind them.

The daily memo from the Fox News management would dictate its "journalists" the angle to take when covering a story and the terminology to use. For example, the term pro-choice is to be replaced with the term pro-abortion even though this is a misleading term.

> You can be against abortion and still be pro-choice.

There were some who worked at Fox News who disobeyed The Memo.

> I cannot in good conscience write _pro-abortion_. I will not write _pro-abortion_. I will write _pro-choice_.

Management reacted to this by sending the disobeying journalist home without pay.

> I was told I could go home for the day. I wouldn't be paid for that day. If that was my hill, then that was the repercussion.

Fox News may have been the most rotten "news" network in America, but the rot existed since the very beginning of cable news. CNN's coverage of the first Gulf War was meant to captivate the viewer with their theatre of tragedy. Informing the viewer was not the primary objective even at CNN.

> Are they reporting on a war or trying to sell me a war?
