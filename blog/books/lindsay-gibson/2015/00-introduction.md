# Introduction

Adults who were emotionally neglected as children will expect other people to save them from the effects of their childhood trauma.

> They build up subconscious fantasies about how other people should act in order to heal them from past neglect.

Making other people responsible for your happiness will only lead to disappointment, frustration, and further unhappiness.

> By grasping the concept of emotional immaturity, you can develop more realistic expectations of other people, accepting the level of relationship possible with them instead of feeling hurt by their lack of response.

Looking for people to save you will land you in the hands of those seeking to benefit from your desperation for emotional support. People will abuse you, and you will let them abuse you because you will think that without them, you will die. A child will die if their parents neglect them, but an adult won't. An adult must realize that their juvenile struggle to find emotional connection with emotionally neglectful people is not the correct way to fill the void left by their parents. It's hard because this struggle has been there your entire early life. You've been desperately reaching for an emotional connection with emotionally neglectful people throughout your childhood, and you felt like you needed it for your survival. Still, you've made it out without it. Sure, the psychological trauma left by the emotional neglect of your parents is there and needs to heal, but you can't heal from it by crying out to your parents or people like them.

> See yourself as a worthy person who is no longer at the mercy of other people’s manipulations.

Emotionally neglectful people will exploit not only your desperation but also your adaptations to it.

> Manage the overdeveloped empathy that may have held you emotionally hostage to manipulative, nonreciprocal people.

Stay away from emotionally neglectful people. Remember that you've survived childhood. Your [inner self] has gotten you through it. Reward it by further developing your [inner world] through learning and imagination. Connect with it on a deeper level through journaling or meditation. Your inner self is your ultimate source for psychological healing. A true connection with another person is a connection of your inner worlds. Find people you can connect with on that level, not those who want to manipulate you.
