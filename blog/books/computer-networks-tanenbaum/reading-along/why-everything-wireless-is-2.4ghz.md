# [Why Everything Wireless Is 2.4 GHz](https://www.wired.com/2010/09/wireless-explainer/)

It's not because it's the optimal frequency for carrying data.

> Routers could function just as well at 2.3 or 2.5 GHz, but they're not allowed. ... For this, they can thank the microwave.

It's because it's the optimal frequency for heating food. Because of the microwaves escaping from the microwave oven at the 2.4 GHz frequency, the surrounding frequency band is not used for emergency communication so it's not regulated by the FCC. Therefore, any device can freely broadcast on it. Thus the proliferation of routers, phones, Bluetooth devices, garage door openers, etc, all on this frequency.
