# Introduction

Emotionally immature people (EIPs) have a desperate need to control others. These are the sorts of people who will shut down any of your attempts at self-discovery.

> EI parents and other EIPs undermine your self-confidence and trust in your intuitions. EI parents and EIPs are hostile toward your inner life by mocking and invalidating your perceptions, thoughts, and feelings.

They will support your studies only because it would allow them to brag about you to their friends. Anything more they will strongly discourage. They may label it learning only for the sake of learning. Why waste your time?

> Be immune to this shaming by staying loyal to your inner experience.

Why waste your time on yourself when your journey of self-improvement would be taking you away from serving them?

> Valuing your inner world is crucial to reestablishing a solid relationship with yourself.

Personal discovery is the most important kind of learning. It teaches you what to do in difficult situations in life.

> Trust yourself and welcome your feelings as invaluable information about what needs your attention.

Depression, anxiety, and other [psychiatric distress] are signs of mental trauma that hasn't healed. Listening to your inner self will allow you to heal.

> Your parents gave you life and love, but only of the sort they knew. You can honor them for that but cease to give them unwarranted power over your emotional well-being. Your mission now is for your own growth: to become an individual who is fully engaged with both yourself and other people.

Only if you develop your [inner world] can you form deep and truly intimate relationships with others. [True intimacy] lies in the connection of the inner worlds of two people.
