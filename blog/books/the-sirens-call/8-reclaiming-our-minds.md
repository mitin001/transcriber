# 8. Reclaiming Our Minds

Corporations are constantly figuring out new ways to monetize the masses. First, they restructured the culture around the workday where labor is extracted from the proletariat at the assembly line for the lowest possible wage.

> Something within us, intimate to us and possessed by us, was transformed, through a series of technological, legal, and market innovations, into a commodity that was extracted from us at a price. This ubiquitous commodification transformed the most granular and private aspects of our entire lives and produced widespread alienation.

Now, they've captured our attention with the algorithm so they can sell it to marketers. In the future, they will sell you remedies for this problem. Mind you, this is the problem of their creation, not yours. And yet, it is still your responsibility to pay them for their solution to this problem.

> Several companies have started to market “dumb phones” that allow the rudiments of cell phone functionality without carrying around a little slot machine in your pocket.

This is akin to AI crawling your content and then spitting it back at you for a price. A fool is born every day. If you don't want to be a fool for corporations, stop buying their bullshit. The real antidote to end-stage corporate capitalism is artisanship. Support small businesses.
