# [Personally Identifiable Information (PII)](https://www.gsa.gov/reference/gsa-privacy-program/rules-and-policies-protecting-pii-privacy-act)

Lawmakers recognize that anything on the Internet can become PII with an advance in technology allowing third parties to use formally anonymous data to identify an individual.

> Non-PII can become PII whenever additional information is made publicly available - in any medium and from any source - that, when combined with other available information, could be used to identify an individual.

Before have recognition, a photo of someone's face wasn't considered to be PII. Now there are search engines that can link anyone's face to the photos they might've posted on social media.
