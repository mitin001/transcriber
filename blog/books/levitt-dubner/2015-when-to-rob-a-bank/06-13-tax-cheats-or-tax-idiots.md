# 06-13. Tax Cheats or Tax Idiots

If the US regains its sanity, there will be a tax reform where the IRS will send every American citizen a bill in January asking them to make up a difference between the tax they owe and the tax they paid last year. The IRS will be like any public utility. But what is the IRS now?

> The IRS is like a street cop or, more precisely, the biggest fleet of street cops in the world, who are asked to enforce laws written by a few hundred people on behalf of a few hundred million people, a great many of whom find these laws too complex, too expensive and unfair.

The government knows exactly how much you owe it, but if you don't happen to arrive at the same number as the government, you will be harshly penalized, regardless of whether you tried to cheat the system or made an honest mistake. And there are plenty of opportunities for mistakes, just as there are hoardes of opportunists that know how to cheat without getting caught. It's a deliberately broken system.
