# 06-01. Cheating to Be Hot

Our society has an appetite for crime.

> Are we too cynical? I don’t think so, but some people do. We routinely hear from readers who say it’s a shame that we’ve called attention to so much deceit, trickery, and cheating.

If our society is driven to stories about con artists and all other kinds of criminals, perhaps it indicates that we live in a cynical society. However, the freakonomists say that the criticisms they receive from the portion of society protesting its descent into cynicism is unfounded.

> Hey, we also called attention to people who don’t cheat.

_Freakonomics_ is by no means cynical, especially by the standards set by the booming (and exploitative) true crime genre and murder porn.
