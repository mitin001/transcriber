# 04-03. Contest: A Six-Word Motto for the U.S.

A Freakonomics blog reader purposes a new motto for America.

> “Our Worst Critics Prefer to Stay” is, while perhaps not outright uplifting, a wonderfully concise acknowledgment of the paradox that a capitalist democracy inevitably is: a place that is often well worth complaining about, and which allows you to complain as loudly as you wish.

It's right up there with Bill Maher's "[capitalism] is the worst economic system, except for all the others." The grass is not greener anywhere else in the world, but be reminded that the grass is only greener if you water it. Take care of this country. Exercise your free speech. When you see something, say something. If you see power being abused, blow the whistle. Protect our institutions.
