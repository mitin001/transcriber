# 06-09. Should We Just Let the Tour de France Dopers Dope Away?

> The history of modern doping began with the cycling craze of the 1890s and the six-day races that lasted from Monday morning to Saturday night. Extra caffeine, peppermint, cocaine, and strychnine were added to the riders' black coffee. Brandy was added to tea. Cyclists were given nitroglycerin to ease back.
