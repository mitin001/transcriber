# *Social Studies*

Fran regards ideas above all else.

> Great people talk about ideas, average people talk about things, and small people talk about wine.

With the proliferation of gossip on television, in magazines, and on social media, I would change the last part to "small people talk about other people." Perhaps such people don't have a choice in the matter. Perhaps they don't want to repeat other people's ideas, so they bored with the unoriginality of ideas, they turn their attention to people instead. There is, after all, no such thing as an original thought. Somebody has already thought up what you think is an original thought.

> Original thought is like original sin: both happened before you were born to people you could not possibly have met.

"You look healthy" is a bad compliment.

> Telling someone he looks healthy isn’t a compliment—it’s a second opinion.

Children shouldn't be calling adults by their first names.

> Never allow your child to call you by your first name. He hasn’t known you long enough.

Adults shouldn't be forcing children into contractual obligations. 

> There isn’t an adult alive who would want to be contractually bound by a decision he came to at the age of nineteen.

Taylor Swift has a lot to say about that.
