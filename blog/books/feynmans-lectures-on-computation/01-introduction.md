# 1. Introduction to Computers

Computers perform the multiplication operation in terms of shift and addition operations on bits.

![17495813351972535165922230836837](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1bad1f06-5b94-4af2-95d8-2cc03441e55d)

They perform addition in terms of xor and carry operations on bits.

> A binary addition of 1 and 1 is 10, which is zero if you forget the carry.

The xor gate outputs 0 if both inputs are the same and 1 if they are different. If addition can be defined in terms of xor and carry, then multiplication can be defined in terms of shift, xor, and carry.
