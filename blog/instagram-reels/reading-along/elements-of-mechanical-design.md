> Stiffening transfers loads to a different place.

It moves the load away from the point susceptible to fracture.

> Structures without triangulating members rely on the rigidity of connecting joints between members for stiffness.

While assembling a bookcase, do not try to move it until the back is attached. Doing so may cause it to collapse.

> It is quite wobbly side-to-side without the back. But once the back is attached, the bookcase becomes remarkably rigid and stable in side-to-side loading.
