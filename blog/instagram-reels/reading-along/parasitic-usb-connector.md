# [Parasitic USB connector](https://web.archive.org/web/20240918190419/http://mg.lol/blog/parasitic-usb-connector/)

A person finds a USB drive laying around, inserts it into the computer, and sees a command prompt window briefly flash on their screen. He panics and swiftly removes the drive from the computer before any real damage is done, that is, before the script finishes executing. What if this person couldn't remove the drive because it physically locked itself into the port until the payload finished running?

> A software-controlled mechanical latch that prevents physical removal of a USB thumb drive.
