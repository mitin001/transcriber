# [Meet the venomous Maine spider that comes out in the winter](https://www.bangordailynews.com/2024/12/26/homestead/homestead-environment/venomous-winter-spider-maine-joam40zk0w/)

Venom from a fishing spider is not dangerous albeit potentially allergenic.

> They carry a venom that can cause an allergic reaction in some people. But that’s not the biggest risk associated with a fishing spider bite. Because they eat freshly killed protein, their fangs are dirty and can have a lot of bacteria living on them. An infection from that bacteria is far more dangerous than the actual venom.

Beware getting an infection from the fangs.
