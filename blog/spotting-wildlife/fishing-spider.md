# Fishing spider

![17431916805546201075978946778435](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/eb5dd4f4-6e6c-4306-828b-d05addc49c81)

Here's a fishing spider I spotted in the Twin Cities area in late March in 2025. These spiders are sexual cannibals like the black widows, and while their bites aren't as dangerous as those of their more famous counterparts, they are to be avoided still for the risk of infection from getting dirty fangs into your skin.
