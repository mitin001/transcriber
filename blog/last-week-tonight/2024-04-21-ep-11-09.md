The term *flying saucer* is misattributed.

> Arnold didn't describe seeing flying saucers, though. What he actually said was the objects flew like a flying saucer, they take a saucer and throw it across the water. But reporters short-handed that to flying saucer and the name stuck.

It should be attributed to the press, not Kenneth Arnold.
