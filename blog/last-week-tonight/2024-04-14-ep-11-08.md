Managed Care Organizations cut costs at the expense of necessary care.

> The most infuriating thing about MCOs is that like with so many players in our for-profit healthcare system, they are incentivized to cut costs at the expense of necessary care. Because MCOs get paid a set monthly amount per person, meaning they get a fixed rate. So, their profit is whatever they don't spend on patients.

What could go wrong?

> In state after state, there have been heartbreaking stories of MCOs denying care and prioritizing cost savings over patients.
