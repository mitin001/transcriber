# LabOfMonsters

Jan 23, 2025.

A Twitter user published an unbelievably rude response to [one of my comments](https://x.com/andysaurusex/status/1882312720013541832). I wanted to understand what would drive someone to speak so rudely on the Internet, so I did OSINT on this user. A Twitter handle is a sufficient input into the OSINT process.

By looking through this person's posts on [Wayback Tweets](https://waybacktweets.streamlit.app/), I saw that he uses his Twitter account to insult people, places, and things perhaps as a way to blow off the frustrations of his daily life. He seems to understand that if his employer discovers this side of him, he may face repercussions detrimental to his professional life. Therein lies his biggest [fear](https://x.com/LabOfMonsters/status/1850945044901216518).

> Some of us have an actual career and aren’t tryna get doxxed.

This comment was part of a Twitter fight, in which a different user, hurt by his insults, called him "[ugly](https://x.com/KatySlaysYrFav/status/1850944059332051271)".

I wanted to respond to this post by linking a post that *LabOfMonsters* made from his former Twitter account where he seems to have called himself [ugly](https://x.com/brazilianbutfab/status/534151577037447168), thereby de-anonymizing *LabOfMonsters* to his adversary, but decided against this as not to dredge up a months-old feud or hurt the person with an intensity that would be disproportionate to his rudeness.

Instead, I wanted to remind him that his cloak of anonymity is paper-thin and can be easily peeled away. Perhaps, this would discourage him from insulting people as much in the future.

Searching for *labofmonsters* reveals accounts on several platforms, including SoundCloud, where this person has revealed some of his [other fears](https://soundcloud.com/labofmonsters/follow-my-new-account-read-description-please-repost): losing his accounts and followers.

> I get an email saying I get a 3rd strike and my soundcloud account is terminated Please follow me on this account and I followed almost everyone I was following before.

On another platform, Tumblr, this person reveals his real name by signing a welcome message with "[Juan](https://web.archive.org/web/20241224010339/https://labofmonsters.tumblr.com/)", linking to his alternate Tumblr blog.

The alternate blog [links](https://juanvevo.tumblr.com/links) to his old Twitter account.

It also betrays his country of origin (Brazil) and his age.

On [April 3, 2014](https://web.archive.org/web/20140325194722/http://juanvevo.com/), he listed his age as 17.

By [May 17, 2014](https://web.archive.org/web/20140517213116/http://juanvevo.com/), he changed it to 18.

Therefore, Juan AKA *LabOfMonsters* was born between April 3 and May 17, 1996.

I used this information to craft my [response](https://x.com/andysaurusex/status/1882340026673221791) to Juan in an attempt to discourage further rudeness from him aimed at people in a way that he thought was anonymous.

> Thanks for that, Juan. 28 years on this planet, and this is how you treat your fellow humans. Not very Brazilian of you.

In response, Juan deleted his original rude response, and six people showed appreciation for my comment.
