# [Lucy Kalanithi: What makes life worth living in the face of death](https://www.ted.com/talks/lucy_kalanithi_what_makes_life_worth_living_in_the_face_of_death/transcript)

Living is learning. To get through painful parts of life, such as grief and loss, focus on what they're teaching you.

> Engaging in the full range of experience -- living and dying, love and loss -- is what we get to do. Being human doesn't happen despite suffering. It happens within it. When we approach suffering together, when we choose not to hide from it, our lives don't diminish, they expand.

Living with pain is playing the game of life on the hard mode. Eventually, you'll get a hang of it and learn to get through it. Your community will help. Share your feelings with them. Grief shared is grief halved.
