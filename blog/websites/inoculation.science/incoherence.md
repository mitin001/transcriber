# [Incoherence](https://inoculation.science/inoculation-videos/incoherence/)

> Multiple arguments are put forward in service of a larger point, and two or more of these arguments are contradictory or rule each other out.

Some people have a limited capacity to reason logically. This prevents them from fully understanding a concept, and in their lack of understanding, they invent incoherent tales to try to sound smarter than they are. Sometimes they overcome their inability to grasp a concept not by working toward understanding it but by giving in to an incoherent conspiracy theory. This allows them to regain a sense of control without too much mental effort.

> There’s no such thing as “scientific consensus”, the issue is still actively debated in the scientific community. Only a few brave scientists dare go against the grain. They’re our heroes, and they should be celebrated.

People who make such claims will also sometimes try to manipulate others into believing them. They will try to baffle people into accepting what they're saying as true.

> The stock market is completely unpredictable. We’re barrelling towards a stock market crash of epic proportions.

Do not try to debate people who make such claims. If you have to say anything, just say that their argument is incoherent and move on. If you agree to debate someone arguing in bad faith, you've already lost. If they were not willing to put in the effort into understanding the subject of debate the first time, don't expect them to try to understand your explanation of it. Expect emotional coercion.
