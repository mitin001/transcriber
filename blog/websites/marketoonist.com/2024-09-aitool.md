# [AI is a Tool](https://marketoonist.com/2024/09/aitool.html)

If you're tempted to throw AI at every problem, remind yourself of Maslow’s hammer (aka the law of the instrument).

> If the only tool you have is a hammer, it is tempting to treat everything as if it were a nail.

![17509719138834229668456911968211](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/adbc0887-1946-49e8-9542-ca49d6fbe4c1)

The law of the instrument is about having a solution and looking for a problem. Or worse yet, trying to jam the problem you actually have into a mold of a problem that fits your solution.

> Like most things in life, the best approach in enterprise is to start not with the technology, but with the business problem you’re trying to solve.

The result is detrimental to your business.

> The kiss of death for enterprise value … is throwing AI at poorly defined problems or problems better suited to non-AI solutions that no one bothered to try.
