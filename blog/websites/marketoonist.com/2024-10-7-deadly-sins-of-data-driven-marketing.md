# [7 Deadly Sins of Data-Driven Marketing](https://marketoonist.com/2024/10/7-deadly-sins-of-data-driven-marketing.html)

Vanity metrics do not do anyone any good. They're distractions. No one cares about how many Facebook friends you have or how many impressions your old blog post received. Do not fall for the empty reassurance of a vanity metric.

> The seductive allure of focusing on data that makes us look good.

Similarly, people who use AI to be more objective need to stop. AI was trained on subjectivity. Asking for it to be objective is inappropriate.

> Data is never just about the data — it’s how we use and make sense of the data. How we interpret the data is subject to the same human biases and foibles that drive everything else we do. Of course AI promises to help with data-driven decision making and yet AI is fueled by many of these same biases and foibles.

Subjectivity does not average out into objectivity. It averages out into biases and foibles.
