![17543390703122474383002554092716](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/fb6bd652-7f23-4025-84e9-3e7da0197732)

A corporate apology is hard, so put in some effort. Do not make ChatGPT write it for you. Find a way to take responsibility and make amends. Be a human being.

> CrowdStrike illustrates some of the tensions at play in any brand crisis — trying to balance responsibility and mitigate liability with incomplete information while still trying to fix the issue.  The path of least resistance is a Mad Libs corporate apology that satisfies no one. Far more effective than an apology is an amend.  It’s not just about saying sorry — it’s about taking responsibility to make things right.
