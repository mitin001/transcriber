# [GenAI Hype](https://marketoonist.com/2024/06/ai-chatbots.html)

In the attention economy, the product doesn't matter. If you want to sell it or monetize it, all you need is attention. True, if a product is good, then people are bound to notice, but not everything good is given attention. If a crappy product has AI in its name, the trendiness of AI alone can carry it. There will be enough attention to embolden development of other crappy products. With the false and dangerous claims in its outputs, it can be argued that AI itself is a crappy product. But much of it is good, or at least, attention-grabbing, so the AI hype rages on.
