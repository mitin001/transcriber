# [Playing it Safe is Risky](https://marketoonist.com/2024/08/safeisrisky.html)

Success is only possible through failure.

> If failure is not an option, then neither is success.

When your idea is stopped on the grounds of being too risky, counter the preemption by highlighting the risks of *not* implementing your idea.

> Organizations can spot the risks of a new idea a mile away. But there’s a curious blind spot when it comes to the risks of not taking those risks.
