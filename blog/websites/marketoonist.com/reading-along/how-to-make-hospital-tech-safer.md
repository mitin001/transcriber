# [How to Make Hospital Tech Much, Much Safer](https://medium.com/backchannel/how-to-make-hospital-tech-much-much-safer-c81dac43684a)

When a doctor prescribed a 38.5x overdose of an antibiotic to a pediatric patient, a pharmacist filled it, and a nurse administered it, none of these healthcare workers got fired over threatening their patient's life in this way.

> One thing we did not do was fire any of the involved clinicians.

Instead, the hospital administrators blamed the system.

> The test we use in such situations is this: Could we imagine another competent individual making the same mistake under the same conditions?

The healthcare system allows for these kinds of catastrophic failures. Hospitals use poorly designed software that blares so many alerts that the truly important ones get missed.
