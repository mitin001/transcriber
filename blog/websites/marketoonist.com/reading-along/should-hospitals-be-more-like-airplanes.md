# [Should Hospitals Be More Like Airplanes?](https://medium.com/backchannel/should-hospitals-be-more-like-airplanes-5d6b9e2067ae)

A society dominated by unnecessary notifications is increasingly made of worn-out, apathetic people. A hospital afflicted by alert fatigue runs an ever-increasing risk of inflicting tragedy on its patients and their loved ones. It was in this state that one hospital staff administered 38.5 pills of Septra to a sick child.

> The overdose triggered a grand mal seizure and Pablo stopped breathing. Within a minute, however, the Code Blue team arrived and was able to revive him.

He survived the overdose. Had the hospital implemented an overdose alert system that wasn't crowded out by hoards of pointless notifications, the overdose could've been avoided in the first place. An effective warning system is one that doesn't alert us needlessly.

> False positives are one of the worst things you could do to any warning system. It just makes people tune them out.
