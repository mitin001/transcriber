# [Cutting through the clutter](https://seths.blog/2016/10/cutting-through-the-clutter/)

If you have something to say, do not say it right away. Keep working on it. Only make your statement when you get it perfect. Otherwise, your message won't stand out enough not to get lost in the clutter.

> You're trying to get through all the noise and the distraction and the clutter with your message. Here's the thing: You are the noise and the distraction and the clutter. Just because it's important to you doesn't mean it's important to us.
