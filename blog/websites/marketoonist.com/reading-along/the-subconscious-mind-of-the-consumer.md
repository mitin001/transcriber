# [The Subconscious Mind of the Consumer](https://www.library.hbs.edu/working-knowledge/the-subconscious-mind-of-the-consumer-and-how-to-reach-it)

You may get deeper, more meaningful insights (better data) from one-on-one conversations than shallow sampling of the crowd. The wisdom of the crowd does not necessarily reveal the most useful information.

> One-on-one interviews are superior to focus groups. That is, even a few conventional one-on-one interviews yield essentially the same data as several focus groups. Additionally, there is now a lot of evidence that personal interviews yield deep insights that can't be obtained from focus groups.
