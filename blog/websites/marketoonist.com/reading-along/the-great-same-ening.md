> ChatGPT, Jasper and all the rest are powerful conformity machines, giving you the ability to churn out Bible-length material about yourself and your business that’s exactly the same as your competitors.

> Every boaster and busybody can now churn out infinite words nobody wants. Words will become an even-more devalued currency.

> Put this on a sticky note on your monitor for when you're reading bold predictions from futurists and other bullshit artists you see on LinkedIn: nothing is the answer to everything.

Google would like me to start with: “Looking for ways to stand out in today’s crowded marketplace? Here are six tips to stand out!” Google wants you to write the first, most obvious idea that pops into your head on any given topic. Applying that thinking to your business helps you create higher margins, because you mean something to your customer. So they won’t drop you the moment they find a lower-price competitor.
