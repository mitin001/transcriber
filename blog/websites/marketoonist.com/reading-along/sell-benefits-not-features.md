# [Sell benefits. Not features](https://x.com/yasminekho/status/1846173195977871669)


Your audience is not made of technical experts on how to solve their problem. That's you. Your job is to solve their problem in a way that's as frictionless for them as possible. Save the explanations for later.


> People don't care about your product. They care about what your product can do for them.

The reason short videos have become so much more popular than long-form content is because people favor quick, frictionless solutions to their problems. Save in-depth explanations for a smaller subset of your audience that wants your guidance, not just your solution.


> Attract an audience with short-form content. Keep the audience with long-form content.

This is similar to the difference between selling a cure and selling prevention. A cure is a simple, straightforward solution to a problem, whereas prevention takes ongoing work, learning, and discipline.


> Selling prevention is hard. Selling a cure is easy.

Who wouldn't dream of losing their handicap and instead gaining an extraordinary ability? Sell this dream.


> Do not order them to buy your stuff. Show them a dream.
