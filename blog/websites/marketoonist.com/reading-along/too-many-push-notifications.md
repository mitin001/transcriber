# [Push Notifications Are Out of Control](https://www.theatlantic.com/technology/archive/2023/05/too-many-push-notifications-apps-phone/674178/)

Poor user interface is not a bug. It is a feature. If you're addicted to the app anyway, its enshittifiers see no downside in making you work harder to get your fix. They will put ads alongside alerts from your kids and doctors and leave you guessing which is which.

> App makers have little incentive to make these controls easy to find or particularly user-friendly, and they have every incentive to find ways to continue stacking their promos in the same list where you get texts from your family and alerts of new test results from your doctor.
