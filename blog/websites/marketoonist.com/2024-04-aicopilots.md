# [AI Co-Pilots](https://marketoonist.com/2024/04/aicopilots.html)

Hiring managers should be asking about the candidates AI complementarity.

> The IMF predicted a few months ago that 60% of jobs in advanced economies will be impacted by AI. They rated jobs in two main categories — “high complementarity” and “low complementarity” to AI.  They forecast that jobs with “high complementarity” to AI would be positively affected by AI and jobs with “low complementarity” would be negatively affected.

My AI complementarity is low. I will ask it to write a script to format data a certain way or write a helper function, but integrating anything into the software project is the top task by time consumption for me.
