# [Thinking Outside the Box](https://marketoonist.com/2024/08/outsidebox.html)

![17515132042132182106110197287466](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9bb82478-7725-40a7-9a45-5667bf188493)

> Engineers thrive on constraints.

How much trouble would you be in if you needed medical care in a rural area before General Electric's MAC 400 ECG was invented?

> GE credits the success of the innovation to tight constraints on cost ($1 a scan), form factor (fits in a backpack), time (18 month development), and budget (one-tenth of the previous product).

Constraints drive innovation.

> To get out of a tight box is to invent your way out.
