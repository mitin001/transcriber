# [Push Notification Fatigue](https://marketoonist.com/2023/05/push-notification-fatigue.html)

Less is more. This can be true for software features.

> Sometimes the best customer experience is less customer experience.

This can be particularly true for notifications.

> The healthcare industry coined the term “Alert Fatigue” to describe the phenomenon of being overwhelmed by the sheer number of safety alerts that the important ones get lost in the clutter. In one cautionary tale, a patient was accidentally given a 3800% order of his medication, and the hospital’s built-in alert system sent preemptive alerts to a doctor and a pharmacist. But both the doctor and pharmacist ignored the system’s alert because the same system generates alerts for 50% of the hundreds of prescriptions they handle each day.

We have gotten so used to dismissing notifications, all notifications have become useless. If you want to communicate something important and you did it in a notification, chances are it was missed, dismissed without attention. This is bad news for the patient who could've been spared potentially life-threatening adverse reactions by the notification that should've alerted their care team of a medication error.
