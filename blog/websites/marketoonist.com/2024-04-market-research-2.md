# [Market Research](https://marketoonist.com/2024/04/market-research-2.html)

People are complicated. They think, feel, talk, and act, but these activities often don't seem to be related. What they think is different from what they feel, and what they say is different from what they do.

> People don’t think what they feel, they don’t say what they think and they don’t do what they say.

Thinking is hard and avoided as much as possible.

> Thinking is to humans as swimming is to cats; they can do it but they’d prefer not to.

People avoid thinking by relying on instinct and emotions. They only start thinking when they need to justify their instincts and emotions.

> How people think is guided mostly by a System 1 mode of thought: fast, instinctive, and emotional. The System 2 mode of thought that is slower, deliberative, and logical is usually playing catch-up — often rationalizing decisions we’ve already made by gut feel.

The need for such justifications doesn't arise frequently in the present-day society.

> 95% of purchasing decisions are subconscious.

It's easy to make people buy things, and marketers have seized upon this.

> People buy on emotion and justify on logic.
