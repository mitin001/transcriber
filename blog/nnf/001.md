# 001

_[Необъяснимо, но факт]_ (ННФ or NNF) [ep. 1] premiered in Russia on March 3, 2005. It was later made available on [RuTube] and [YouTube]. The video transcript auto-generated by YouTube is still available on [Filmot].

The episode featured clips of historical footage of the Tunguska event, likely taken from a documentary (e.g., [Cosmos (1980)]). NNF was notorious for taking clips from other documentaries (e.g., BBC's _The Human Body_ or _Walking with Dinosaurs_) and passing them as archival footage.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/bc59f1b3-4bf0-4a50-a5f1-bc8a673605f9

In this episode, the Tunguska footage of Tunguska is juxtaposed with historical footage of the nuclear weapon tests, perhaps taken from a documentary or maybe even a music video.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/a7641f15-2fb9-45ff-960f-a3a65e2358e3

The video for _Red Socks Pugie_ by Foals is one example of a work that uses archival stock footage from the 1960s (e.g., animations of the human circulatory system from [Pond5] or [ShutterStock] and excerpts of [propaganda] films about communist China that show the nukes that can also be obtained from ShutterStock or [Videvo]). _Red Socks Pugie_ came out in 2008, three years after NNF ep.1, so it couldn't have been the source of the nukes there.

Music lovers seem to have an outsized interest in the nukes. For example, in 2005, rocker Vampiria discussed gothic, black, and death metal on a [music forum], signing each message with an avatar that plays a gif of a clip from the above. In 2006, YouTuber lakeeriemonster uploaded a [video] for _So Much Beauty In Dirt_ by Modest Mouse also featuring the clips from the above.

The abundance of borrowed material in NNF is often paired with the fruits of some original cinematic experimentation. In this episode, the NNF team imagined a tunnel of light seen by people undergoing the near-death experience.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/87418607-9349-49c8-b945-357d9488481a

Some footage used in this episode is hard to classify as original or borrowed. The desert storm does not look like an original work, but attempts to do a reverse image search on its frames have also proven fruitless.
