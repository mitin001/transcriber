# [A Reader’s Digest of the Avant-Garde](https://www.filmcomment.com/article/independents-amos-vogel-avant-garde/)

Amos Vogel warns that when confronted with a work of art, people will see what they want to see in it even if it's not there.

> The enforced revelation of non-existing patterns based on preconceived ideas is not history but metaphysics.

*South Park* gave us a brilliant illustration of this when as a joke their elementary school kids wrote the most disgusting, filthy novel they could imagine, and their parents thought it was genius writing worthy of the Nobel Prize in literature.
