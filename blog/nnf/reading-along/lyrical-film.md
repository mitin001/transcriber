# [The Lyrical Film](https://books.google.com/books?id=vlwU-MYE5pQC)

The lyrical film makes it possible for one person to make a complete film. He is the camera operator as well as the actor. He is also the editor. He invites you to see the world as he saw it, and that is the film.

> The lyrical film postulates the film-maker behind the camera as the first-person protagonist of the film. The images of the film are what he sees, filmed in such a way that we never forget his presence and we know how he is reacting to his vision.

To distract the viewer from the cuts between shots, he makes use of plastic cutting.

> The joining of shots at points of movement, close-up, or abstraction to soften the brunt of montage.
