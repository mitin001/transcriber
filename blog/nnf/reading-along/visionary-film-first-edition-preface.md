# Preface to the first edition of Sitney's *[Visionary Film](https://books.google.com/books?id=vlwU-MYE5pQC)*

Sitney is drawn to film-makers who have such an all-encompassing need to express themselves through film that they denounce earthly possessions, sacrifice them to their craft. The pursuit of film-making lands them in poverty.

> The film-makers in question, like poets, produce their work without financial reward, often making great personal sacrifices to do so.

Sitney purposes four categories for the products of their labor.

> The trance film, the mythopoeic film, the structural film, and the participatory film.
