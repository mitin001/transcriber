# Wendy Williams [Larry King Interview 2013](https://youtube.com/watch?v=Pf_h8kKG0T4)

[Transcript](https://s3.amazonaws.com/writecomments.com/transcripts/4724411611490168747382bb3af574f0.csv).

> As embarrassing as it might be to a lot of people, many people care about pop culture and the nonsense.

Wendy Williams sees pop culture as a rich source of ice breakers for those of us who don't have the gift of being able to work a crowd.

> It's light conversation that often people need because they might not be good communicators. Do you realize how special it is for people like me and you? We possess something. We can go to a room by ourselves and totally start a conversation with anybody.

Hot topics from pop culture do not threaten people as much as political opinions.

> Hot topics are a very common non-threatening thing to mention. You go to a dinner party and you talk about Syria. Well, now you have political opinions.

Pop culture can be a gateway into a conversation.

> But to say, you know, how about that Miley Cyrus? Well, now we got a conversation. That's watercooler stuff. It's watercooler stuff, but that's how you get a conversation going. A light conversation leads to a deeper one. Do you know how many people out there are socially inept?
