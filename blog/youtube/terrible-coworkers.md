# [Bill Burr on Dealing With Terrible Coworkers](https://youtube.com/watch?v=WWOqxxklKWE)

[Transcript](https://s3.amazonaws.com/writecomments.com/transcripts/2319bd7e61585a150ced3795f0fa9da1.csv).
  
Bill Burr was on _Love Line_ with Dr. Drew when a woman that sounded like a cartoon character called into the show complaining of an intimacy problem she had with her boyfriend.

> My boyfriend tried to have sex with me. It like really hurts.

The first question Dr. Drew asked her was about her childhood trauma.

> Right off the bat he goes, "What happened to you when you were six?"

Dr. Drew explains that he got the idea to go there from the way she makes her voice sound.

> When I heard her,	I heard a six-year-old.

The childhood trauma was so overwhelming it resulted in arrested development. The girl never learned to talk like an adult even when she became an adult. Bill Burr says that even without this knowledge, meeting a person like this would be creepy.

> There's a whole new level of being creeped out when, you know, you see girls like that.

Now that he understands that people like that are in a state of arrested development, it's even creepier. This is a child in an adult body.

> You go into their fucking bedroom, and it's like a little girl's room.

A listener writes to Bill Burr asking about a coworker who surrounds himself with Batman and Mickey Mouse memorabilia. Bill Burr applies what he's learned on _Love Line_.

> He had fucking dreamed of comic book when he was nine. So now he has this arrested development that now he's actually fucking at work and he's sitting in there doing this adult job, but in his head just trying to block out the trauma that happened in his head. He just has just playing on a loop, non-fucking-stop, Batman.
