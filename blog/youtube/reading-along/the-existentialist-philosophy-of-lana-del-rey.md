# [The existentialist philosophy of Lana Del Rey](https://theconversation.com/the-existentialist-philosophy-of-lana-del-rey-260131)

When people find themselves to be alive, they find that being alive comes with responsibility.

> Responsibility to create meaning for their existence by living life as a work of art.

Challenge the assertion of the meaninglessness of your existence by leaving behind a work of art.
