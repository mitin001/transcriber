# [Going Gaga](https://books.google.com/books?vid=9780785234333) by Laurieann Gibson

When she listened to Paparazzi by Lady Gaga for the first time, Laurieann Gibson heard through this song Gaga's deep, obsessive, all-consuming desire for fame. To be famous against all odds and despite all the scrutiny and danger fame brings with it.

> It’s a love song about your dream. The paparazzi, the fame.
