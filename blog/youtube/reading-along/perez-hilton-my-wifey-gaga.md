# [My Wifey](https://books.google.com/books?vid=9781641604062) by Perez Hilton

Musicians have managers, and managers have assistants. The assistants do the grunt work of reaching out to the press or media producers for promotional opportunities. Troy Carter was Lady Gaga's manager, and he had his assistant reach out to blogger Perez Hilton.

> Troy’s assistant asked whether I would be interested in listening to a few of her tracks, ending one email with “She’s amazing.”

Perez notes that assistants are paid to hype up the musicians they represent even if they personally don't believe in them or have the instincts to judge what makes a great musician.

> The assistant had—maybe without even really knowing it—been telling the truth.
